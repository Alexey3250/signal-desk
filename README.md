# Signal Desk

Signal Desk is an analytics portfolio: sector-focused dashboards and market
studies that turn raw data into business insight.

## Structure

- Automotive
- Aviation
- Real Estate
- Finance / Macro
- Product Analytics

## First project

**UAE Automotive Pricing Intelligence** is a market analysis case study reviewing
pricing patterns, segment trends, and business implications for dealers and
platforms.

## Positioning

Built Signal Desk, a Next.js analytics portfolio publishing market intelligence
dashboards across automotive, aviation, and real estate.
